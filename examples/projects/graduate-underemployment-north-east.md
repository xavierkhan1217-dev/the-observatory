Reported graduate employment rates treat all employment identically. This project attempts to separate graduate-level employment from employment generally, for graduates in the North East, and to track how that gap changes over the first five years after graduation.

## The question

How much of reported graduate employment is in roles that require a degree, and how persistent is the gap for those who start out underemployed?

## Method

1. Classify destination occupations by qualification requirement using standard occupational codes.
2. Compare the resulting graduate-level employment rate against the headline figure.
3. Track cohort persistence: what share of those underemployed in year one remain so in year five.

## Status

Data collection and method design. We intend to publish the method note before the results, so that the approach can be criticised independently of what it finds.
