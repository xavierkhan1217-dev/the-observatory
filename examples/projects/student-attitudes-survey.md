Student political attitudes are usually reported through party identification, which is a poor proxy for what people actually believe about specific institutional and distributive questions. This survey is designed to measure the preferences directly and then examine how well identity predicts them.

## The question

How closely do students' stated political identities track their answers on concrete questions about institutions, redistribution and the legitimacy of unelected decision-making?

## Design

- A preference battery covering institutional trust, redistribution, and delegation to independent bodies.
- Identity questions asked **after** the preference battery, to limit priming.
- Pre-registered hypotheses published before fieldwork begins.

## Status

Design stage. Questionnaire and pre-registration to be published before any data is collected.
